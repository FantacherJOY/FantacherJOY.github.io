Md Fantacher Islam

PhD Candidate, Systems & Industrial Engineering
University of Arizona

I am a PhD candidate in the Health Systems Engineering & Informatics Laboratory at the University of Arizona. My work applies health informatics, causal inference, and clinical machine learning to real-world health data from MIMIC-IV, the NIH RECOVER Cohort, and the All of Us Research Program, to improve clinical decision support and health outcomes.

Research Interests

I am interested in causal inference and representation learning for longitudinal health data. I care about methods that are reproducible, and interpretable.

Areas of Interest: Health Informatics; Clinical Decision Support; Causal Inference; Digital Twins; Biomedical Ontologies; Long COVID; EHR & Clinical Data; MIMIC-IV; All of Us; eICU

Advisor

I work under the supervision of Dr. Vignesh Subbian in the Department of Systems and Industrial Engineering at the University of Arizona.
